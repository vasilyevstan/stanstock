# Accepted Forecast Direction and Release Gates

> Release state: see [README](../README.md#release-status).

This document replaces the old staged fundamental-first roadmap and the old
02:00 scheduling proposal. The binding `research-product-v1` direction is a
price-research product with a 03:30 Europe/Tallinn schedule. Frozen historical
methods remain archives; they are not redefined here.

## Accepted product

The primary authenticated research experience uses:

1. `us-relative-momentum-v1` for one 126-session decision; and
2. `us-price-fhs-v1` for advisory 126-, 252-, 756-, and 1260-session
   projections.

No SEC input is required. There is no fitted model, overall score, probability
of gain, calibrated confidence, fair value, or automatic trade.

Each qualified listing produces one analysis and exactly five immutable
prediction rows. See [Price research](price-research.md).

## Release gates still required

The orchestrator must bind each result to the final exact head:

1. finish the integrated source corrections and local validation;
2. run independent research-integrity review;
3. run adversarial critic-tester review and the complete regression/CI set;
4. complete separate multi-model documentation clarity/privacy review;
5. perform the private fixed-denominator usefulness acceptance;
6. generate/register real retrospective evidence, retaining empty and
   worse-than-baseline results;
7. prove serving and scheduler profile alignment;
8. prove clean-revision/on-time issuance safety; and
9. obtain final-validator release acceptance.

No fixture count, simulated path count, code-coverage percentage, or
calculation success alone satisfies these gates.

## Retrospective evidence plan

The frozen protocol:

- starts its anchor grid at 2019-09-03;
- requires 756 prior returns at every anchor;
- spaces anchors by evaluated horizon;
- places development outcomes entirely before 2024;
- places validation anchors and complete outcomes within 2024;
- places holdout anchors on/after 2025-01-01 with outcomes complete through
  2026-09-11;
- purges intervals crossing partitions; and
- forbids tuning on the final holdout.

FHS is compared on identical paired support against two Gaussian baselines:
zero log drift and historical log drift. Means are calculated within target
cohorts before equal-cohort aggregation.

The report retains empty long partitions, unavailable comparisons, and
candidate-worse results. Numerical 16,384-path convergence checks are
engineering diagnostics, not financial observations.

## Frozen archive boundary

Historical records keep their original identities and meanings, including:

- `default-v1`;
- `us-price-baseline-v1/v2/v3`;
- `us-price-medium-v1/v2`;
- `us-sec-long-v1/v2/v3/v4`; and
- their original opportunity, outcome, and performance policies.

The active reader never silently substitutes these methods into a new product
card. Archives preserve their config bytes/hashes, reason wording, successful
and withheld payloads, immutable predictions, and source vintages.

## Deferred work

Not part of this release:

- fitted ML or parameter fitting;
- fundamental repair or a new active fundamental forecast;
- broad cheap-stock discovery;
- European live equity prices;
- another provider;
- automatic trading;
- brokerage/tax ingestion;
- portfolio or simulation methodology redesign; and
- distributed scheduling/queues or a second analytics/web framework.

Any material method change requires a new version and a new reviewed contract;
it cannot be smuggled into `research-product-v1`.
