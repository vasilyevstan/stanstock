Transparent by design

Methodology and limitations

StanStock uses deterministic rules, versioned weights, immutable data vintages, and out-of-sample evaluation. It does not use an LLM to alter scores or recommendations.

Point in time

Every live result is limited to assets retrieved and available by its decision time. Later restatements and adjusted histories cannot rewrite past inputs.

Scoring

Each available raw factor is independently mapped to 0-100 by a fixed affine or piecewise policy map, clamped, averaged into a component, and combined with versioned horizon weights. Opportunity order uses the completed overall score; it is not factor normalization.

YAML/config policy is bound by the stored configuration hash; code-defined transforms are identified by the stored code_revision. Scheduled observed production automatically binds an exact clean commit SHA. Demo and direct research can record working-tree unless an exact committed revision is explicitly supplied. Missing data reduces the heuristic evidence score instead of becoming zero. rsi_14 uses simple averages of the latest 14 gains and losses (Cutler/SMA style), and its score transform is a StanStock heuristic, not a literature-standard RSI transform.

Scenarios

For 6m/12m forecasts, matched and unconditional cohort-weighted p20, p50, and p80 estimates are each shrinkage blended. Bear/base/bull are the blended p20/p50/p80.

Bear-to-bull is a nominal central 60% analog-return range, not a calibrated prediction, credible, or confidence interval; it has no coverage guarantee. The positive-return estimate is withheld unless its publication gate passes, and a pass does not prove calibrated probabilities.

Long horizons

The bounded tax input is a GAAP accrual proxy: TTM income-tax expense divided by TTM pretax income, not cash taxes paid or a cash tax rate. Reinvestment is compatible balance-sheet invested-capital change divided by NOPAT, an accounting proxy rather than observed capex or a proven causal rate.

Three- and five-year cases use separate frozen horizon-specific fade and multiple-reversion paths; neither is a slice or extrapolation of one shared five-year path.

Principles and fixed policy

Broad research principles include momentum/trend, explicit risk, base rates and shrinkage, sustainable-growth accounting, valuation mean reversion, and point-in-time evaluation. Exact lookbacks, maps, RSI transform, weights, buckets, caps, support/confidence formulas, fallbacks, thresholds, and gates are StanStock policy—not literature-standard, optimized, causal, or statistically calibrated constants.

Limitations

Free sources can be incomplete, delayed, revised, or unavailable. European filing coverage and total-return data may be less complete than US price history.
