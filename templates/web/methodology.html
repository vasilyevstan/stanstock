Transparent by design

Methodology and limitations

StanStock uses deterministic rules, versioned weights, immutable data vintages, and out-of-sample evaluation. It does not use an LLM to alter scores or recommendations.

Version-qualified horizon methods

The exact us-price-baseline-v1, us-price-baseline-v2, and us-price-baseline-v3 configurations use a Short decision — 1–10 trading days method as the sole source of BUY/HOLD/AVOID and the displayed score, plus canonical Medium advisory — 6 months and 12 months and Long advisory — 3 years and 5 years methods.

The exact default-v1 configuration is a frozen legacy decision method. Its displayed overall score is medium-weighted; quality, growth, valuation, momentum/technical, risk/liquidity, and market/sector evidence can contribute. BUY uses configured legacy short, medium, and long downside gates, and the run persists legacy medium/long decision evidence.

The canonical methods are not votes or blends, and unequal-horizon cumulative returns are not directly comparable. Legacy default-v1 evidence is not retrospectively canonical advisory evidence.

Point in time

Every live result is limited to assets retrieved and available by its decision time. Later restatements and adjusted histories cannot rewrite past inputs.

Scoring

Each available raw factor is independently mapped to 0-100 by a fixed affine or piecewise policy map, clamped, averaged into a component, and combined with versioned horizon weights. Opportunity order uses the completed overall score; it is not factor normalization.

YAML/config policy is bound by the stored configuration hash; code-defined transforms are identified by the stored code_revision. Scheduled observed production automatically binds an exact clean commit SHA. Demo and direct research can record working-tree unless an exact committed revision is explicitly supplied. Missing data reduces the heuristic evidence score instead of becoming zero. rsi_14 uses simple averages of the latest 14 gains and losses (Cutler/SMA style), and its score transform is a StanStock heuristic, not a literature-standard RSI transform.

Short v3 research policy

us-price-baseline-v3 is explicit research use only; scheduled/live US analysis, the default selector, and medium/long lanes remain v2. V3 persists one short decision prediction. Generic latest readers remain method-neutral, so an explicitly created v3 row may still become the displayed latest result; that is not default activation.

Its strict YAML is the sole authority for 16 maps (8 momentum/technical, 5 risk/liquidity, 3 market). Cutler/SMA RSI uses the latest 14 changes and maps continuously as H(RSI; 30, 70), so 30/50/70 becomes 0/50/100; it is not Wilder RSI. Positive abnormal volume uses clamp(100 - 20 × |x - 1|), but a reported ratio at or below zero scores zero. The resulting jump from zero to a right-hand limit of 80 is intentional.

Volatility, downside deviation, drawdown, and beta use exactly the latest 252 common closes (251 aligned return pairs) with matching latest dates. The separate 252-session relative return needs 253 overlapping closes. Beta is excluded from factor and conviction scores and appears only as the absolute-beta penalty in a four-penalty risk average. If any penalty is unavailable, risk is visibly insufficient and BUY is blocked, while an empirical scenario and independent AVOID gate may remain numeric/active.

OHLC/close × k together with share volume ÷ k preserves normalized decisions; raw price indicators scale with k. Price × k with fixed volume instead multiplies measured USD turnover and may change the liquidity factor, score, $5m BUY gate, and recommendation. Price bands never enter this arithmetic. Provider-reported volume has not been proven split-compatible.

V3 source and observed-use boundary

Each persisted v3 listing and each requested benchmark is selected once through AsOfData, then that exact immutable asset is physically read and checksum-verified once. Omission is 0 selections/0 reads and explicit common-risk insufficiency. Requested-missing is 1 failed selection/0 reads; selected-corrupt is 1 selection/1 attempted read. Failures propagate and roll back owned output—there is no cache, fallback, substitute, reselection, or downgrade. V3 is USD-only and records exact asset UUID/checksum/provider/subject and retrieval/availability timestamps.

An exceptional observed v3 issuance must be a direct analyze_snapshot(..., issued_on_time=True, ...) call, never manage.py analyze. For that explicit observed-v3 request, the service enforces the exact v3 config version and effective config hash, Twelve Data, SPY, and a raw lowercase 40-hex revision equal to the checkout's exact clean committed HEAD. The caller still owns reviewed-production-universe selection and independent pre-invocation proof of the next-session deadline and source-cutoff safety; existing service checks remain fail-closed. Generation time, target, cutoff, and retrieval time remain distinct.

Scenarios

For 6m/12m forecasts, matched and unconditional cohort-weighted p20, p50, and p80 estimates are each shrinkage blended. Bear/base/bull are the blended p20/p50/p80.

Bear-to-bull is a nominal central 60% analog-return range, not a calibrated prediction, credible, or confidence interval; it has no coverage guarantee. The positive-return estimate is withheld unless its publication gate passes, and a pass does not prove calibrated probabilities.

Long horizons

Legacy long V1-V3 use a bounded tax input that is a GAAP accrual proxy: TTM income-tax expense divided by TTM pretax income, not cash taxes paid or a cash tax rate. Their reinvestment input is compatible balance-sheet invested-capital change divided by NOPAT, an accounting proxy rather than observed capex or a proven causal rate.

Only legacy V1-V3 use separate frozen horizon-specific fade and multiple-reversion paths; their three- and five-year cases are not slices or extrapolations of one shared five-year path.

us-sec-long-v4 is a separate research-only, unactivated, deterministic, and uncalibrated method. It uses reported-GAAP entity FCF, permits a genuine-absence-only net-income fallback, and then applies exactly one diluted-share translation. V4 uses one coherent five-year path: 3y is year 3 and 5y is year 5.

V4 confidence and positive-return probability are unavailable. It makes no intangible-capitalization, ROIC/ROIIC, reinvestment, causal-return, or return-on-new-capital claim.

Principles and fixed policy

Broad research principles include momentum/trend, explicit risk, base rates and shrinkage, sustainable-growth accounting, valuation mean reversion, and point-in-time evaluation. Wilder (1978), Jegadeesh and Titman (1993), Moskowitz, Ooi, and Pedersen (2012), Sharpe (1964), and Amihud (2002) provide broad context—not validation of StanStock's formulas or profitability. Exact lookbacks, maps, RSI transform, weights, buckets, caps, support/confidence formulas, fallbacks, thresholds, and gates are StanStock policy—not literature-standard, optimized, causal, or statistically calibrated constants, and they are not guaranteed. Dollar volume is not the Amihud measure.

For V4, Damodaran and Nissim-Penman provide valuation and accounting context; Fama-French and Bhojraj-Lee/Bhojraj-Lee-Oler cover industry and peer classification; Vorst-Yohn covers forecast-input interpretation; FASB Statement 2 / ASC 730 and ASC 260 define relevant reported accounting; Lev-Sougiannis, Peters-Taylor, and Ewens-Peters-Wang motivate the intangible-capital nonclaim; and Lo-MacKinlay supplies a data-snooping caution. These sources do not validate StanStock's constants, forecast accuracy, alpha, or profitability.

Limitations

Free sources can be incomplete, delayed, revised, or unavailable. European filing coverage and total-return data may be less complete than US price history.
