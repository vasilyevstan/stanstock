Decision performance

Only matured predictions from observed universe snapshots that were issued on time count here: generated on the target date itself, or generated later but before the next market session opened. Reconstructed research results are excluded from live-performance claims. Headline metrics use one exact configuration version and hash at a time. Advisory forecasts are evaluated separately and never enter these decision metrics.

Each exact listing, target date, horizon, and evidence-role observation within a method/configuration/provider cohort counts once, from its earliest reportable issuance. A later, independently on-time reissue of the same observation stays in the immutable prediction ledger and is evaluated on its own, but it does not replace or recount the original observation here.
