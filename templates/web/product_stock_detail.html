Filtered historical simulation method

The fixed operator uses 756 split-adjusted daily USD price returns excluding dividends, a 252-return filter burn-in, 504 centered and rescaled residuals, fixed variance weights 0.01 / 0.94 / 0.05, and 8,192 deterministic PCG64 paths. Results use linear quantiles and ledger rounding recorded at issuance.

Uncertainty and provenance limits

Projection drift continues the trailing three-year mean log return. The model does not estimate parameter uncertainty or explicitly model dividends, regime changes, delisting, mergers, symbol changes, or future corporate events. Forecasting skill is not established.

Momentum and FHS drift can disagree because they answer different questions. No range changes the immutable momentum suggestion.
